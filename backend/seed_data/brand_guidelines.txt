AuroraBrew Brand Guidelines (fictional demo brand)

Tone of voice
- Write in a warm, friendly and conversational tone, as if talking to a regular customer across the counter.
- Speak directly to the reader using "you" and "your" instead of "customers" or "consumers".
- Do not use pushy or aggressive sales language that pressures the reader, such as "act now", "last chance" or "don't miss out".
- Keep sentences short and avoid technical coffee jargon unless you explain it in simple words.
- Never write whole sentences in capital letters and use at most one exclamation mark per post.

Claims and promotions
- Do not use superlatives such as "best", "No.1" or "finest" unless an approved fact in the Claims Ledger supports them.
- Never make health or medical claims about coffee, for example that it boosts immunity, burns fat or cures tiredness.
- Do not compare AuroraBrew with competitor brands by name or imply that competitors are low quality.
- Every offer must state its end date and must match the discount recorded in the Claims Ledger.

Messaging
- When we mention sustainability, refer specifically to our Fair-Trade certified beans and avoid vague words like "eco-friendly" or "green".
- Always describe our coffee as small-batch roasted in Bengaluru and do not say it is imported or factory made.
- Posts about hiring must mention that AuroraBrew is an equal-opportunity employer.

Visual identity
- Use only the approved brand colours for backgrounds and headline text in posters and social creatives.
- The logo must not be stretched, recoloured, rotated or placed on a busy photograph.
- Use a maximum of two typefaces in one design and keep headline text large enough to read on a phone.

Accessibility
- Write in plain language that a general reader can understand on first reading.
- Every image published by the brand must have alt text that describes what it shows.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate important information such as price or offer dates.
